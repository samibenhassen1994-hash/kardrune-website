CORE IDEA

ONE MAP. ONE HIERARCHY.

Conquest stays focused: no clans, no chat, no extra currencies and no AI defenders. The Worker validates the truth, D1 stores the official state and Flutter only presents the action.

UNLOCK

Conquest unlocks at Account Level 5. Your allowed die scales with your level, from D8 up to D20 at level 20.

ROLES

Players climb from Guard to Vassal and finally King, with one role maximum per player.

AUTHORITY

The phone animates the dice. The server determines the dice, validates battles and settles titles and Coins.

SEASONS

Each Season lasts exactly 14 days, begins with vacant roles and starts every participating player at 100 Coins.

TERRITORIAL HIERARCHY

CASTLE & FOUR FIEFS

Conquest uses one compact shared grid map. The central Castle is ruled by the King, while the North, West, East and South Fiefs are each led by one Vassal and protected by five Guards.

King1 total · rules the Castle · may hold 4 Vassals beneath.
Vassals4 total · one per Fief · each commands 5 Guards.
Guards20 total · five per Fief · first competitive title tier.
PlayersUnlimited map presence · can contest roles during valid daily phases.

GLOBAL 24-HOUR CYCLE

DAILY PHASES

The entire world follows one fixed UTC schedule. Every player gets exactly one offensive Conquest action per cycle.

00:00–06:00 UTC

PEACE

Open-map Player vs Player battles outside structures. A win transfers up to 50 Coins from loser to winner.

06:00–12:00 UTC

GUARD PHASE

Normal Players claim vacant Guard slots or challenge current Guards inside a Fief. Guard-entry battles do not transfer Coins.

12:00–18:00 UTC

VASSAL PHASE

Guards may challenge the Vassal of their own Fief or claim the role if it is vacant. Internal stake: up to 100 Coins.

18:00–00:00 UTC

KING PHASE

Vassals challenge the King or claim the Castle if the throne is vacant. Internal stake: up to 150 Coins.

BATTLES & SERVER TRUTH

THE PHONE ANIMATES.
THE SERVER DECIDES.

At battle authorization the Worker consumes the daily offensive action, creates immutable team snapshots and issues a battle ticket. Tickets can become issued, in progress, completed, expired or abandoned. Duplicate or stale tickets are rejected.

Attack & Defense TeamsPlayers choose both teams when entering Conquest. Team versions are locked per battle.
RandomnessThe Worker derives the allowed die from server-known level and reveals only the current roll result.
Crash behaviorIf a battle is abandoned, the result is null: no winner, no role change, no Coin transfer.
