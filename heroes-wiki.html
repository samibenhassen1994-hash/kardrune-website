This growing KardRune encyclopedia introduces published heroes and enemies with character art already used across the official website. How many stages does a KardRune hero have?

Heroes can evolve across three stages. The art and exact evolution names shown here are limited to information already established for the game.

Not yet. This initial public encyclopedia covers heroes and enemies with existing website artwork and will be expanded as more entries are verified.
